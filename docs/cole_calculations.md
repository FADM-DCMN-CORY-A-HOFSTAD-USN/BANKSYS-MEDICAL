## **Biophysical Data Analysis**

> 1. **The Low-Frequency Membrane Shield (10 Hz):** At 10 Hz, the calculated real relative permittivity values are incredibly large, reaching millions of units in tissues like **Grey Matter (\$\\varepsilon\_r \= 4.61 \\times 10^6\$)**. This occurs because long relaxation times (\$\\tau\_1\$) allow slow ion currents to pile up along the insulating phospholipid head group layers, creating massive interfacial polarization fields \[Gabriel et al., 1996\]. At this frequency, total conductivity is almost entirely dictated by the baseline static ionic leakage (\$\\sigma\_s\$).  
> 2. **The Microwave Free-Water Window (2.45 GHz):** When the system scales to 2.45 GHz, cellular membranes are no longer fast enough to stop the field lines. Permittivity numbers drop to double digits as the dielectric response transitions to free water molecules \[Gabriel et al., 1996\]. The high apparent conductivity in grey matter at 2.45 GHz (\$\\sigma \= 16.93\\text{ S/m}\$) is driven by the rapid, friction-induced rotation of water dipoles matching the intense spatial frequency coordinates of the signal \[Gabriel, 1996; Sartori & Lloyd, 2014\].
